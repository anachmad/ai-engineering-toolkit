# ADR standard v1

Use the approved eight headings and metadata in `standards/adr-v1.json`. Preserve existing decision IDs and records. Do not invent options, approvals or evidence.

## Metadata & Status

ID, judul, status keputusan, pemilik, tanggal dan rujukan persetujuan yang tersedia.

## Context

Masalah, batasan dan keadaan yang mendorong keputusan.

## Decision Drivers

Kebutuhan, atribut kualitas, risiko dan pertimbangan yang memengaruhi pilihan.

## Considered Options

Pilihan yang sungguh dipertimbangkan; jangan mengarang alternatif.

## Decision

Keputusan yang dipilih atau usulan yang masih menunggu keputusan.

## Rationale & Trade-offs

Alasan pilihan serta manfaat, kelemahan dan kompromi.

## Consequences

Dampak, risiko, tindak lanjut dan hal yang masih belum diketahui.

## Related Requirements & References

Tautan ke requirement, SAD, ADR terkait, sumber dan bukti yang relevan.

## Validation and review

The template contains a Field/Value metadata table. Retain existing project ID conventions; ADR-001 is the default for new formats. Decision status and document status are separate. Use TBD when unknown. Only the supersession metadata may use `Not applicable — reason`; section-level non-applicability also requires a reason. Dates must be real dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. A status is a claim, not independent proof of approval. IDs in other documents, semantic quality, links not checked, approver identity and evidence remain subject to review. Validator checks do not imply compliance or release authorization. Existing ADRs are not automatically migrated.
